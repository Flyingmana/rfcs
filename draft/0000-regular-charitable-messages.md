# Regular Charitable Messages

## Summary

Use our Reach to make aware of charitable Topics

## Motivation

Privilege is beeing able to ignore a topic. Doing good is, to do something anyway.
Seeing that some people feel very hurt by it, and degrade such software as "protestware" is a clear sign for it having an effect.

## Detailed Explanation

We have a certain reach, and we have a few options how we could use this reach to support awareness about topics. And there are a few topics, which actually do affect many of us in various ways.
Some of this affecting our actual livinghood.

The War against the Ukraine is one of them. Which is why we already did include one message, and got at least one very complaining response on Social Media describing us as "protestware" and that he doesnt want to use such software.
And with it going longer and longer, it is probably justified to do another one.

Another potential topic likely going to affect many of us in the longrun is climate change.



## Rationale and Alternatives

### Alternative 1

Do a Yearly Message over the OpenMage notifications

### Alternative 2

Do a Yearly Message over our Blog

### Alternative 3

have a banner in OpenMage Admin area with the latest current topic, which can be hidden by users till the topic changes.
